# Guideline comparison provenance and maintenance

The independent collection in `data/headache_guidelines.json` is an editorial comparison of official guidance. It is **not** generated by the daily PubMed/AI pipeline and is not subject to the paper collection's JIF or publication-window rules. Its review date must never be refreshed merely because the paper sync ran.

Reviewed **2026-10-18**. Coverage: Taiwan, Japan, nine European countries (United Kingdom, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Denmark, Switzerland, Finland), plus EHF/EAN. NICE and Scottish SIGN are separate jurisdictions. This is not an exhaustive inventory of Europe or of every ICHD syndrome. Migraine has the broadest coverage; cluster headache and trigeminal neuralgia contain only individually verified comparisons. An absent jurisdiction-topic pair is a coverage gap, not a claim that the country lacks guidance.

## Version checks

Check the current official index **and** the document's own publication/version information. Website upload dates, search-engine dates, surveillance announcements and planned revisions are not new clinical guidelines.

- Taiwan: [THS](https://taiwanheadache.org.tw/) and original publications [acute 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42723131/), [prevention 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36089629/). These have different scopes; the acute update does not supersede preventive rankings. Our treatment extraction is limited to their original abstracts.
- Japan: [JHS index](https://www.jhsnet.net/guideline.html), comprehensive 2021 guideline and 2025-10-27 CGRP position statement. Keep the first-line clinical position separate from the optimal-use criteria quoted in the statement.
- UK: [NICE CG150](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg150) (core last updated 2025-06-03; technology-appraisal links can be newer); [SIGN/NHS toolkit](https://www.rightdecisions.scot.nhs.uk/pharmacological-management-of-migraine-sign/) explicitly identifies the 2026 update. A planned NICE prevention revision is not an implemented recommendation.
- Germany: [DMKG guidelines](https://www.dmkg.de/leitlinien/), DGN S1 v7.1, comprehensive revision 2025-08-08 and correction 2025-11-06. The PDF states validity through 2028-08-07.
- France: [SFEMC recommendations](https://sfemc.fr/professionnels/recommandations/) retain 2021 guidance; the 2024 online / 2025 journal position paper is a partial update, posted by SFEMC on 2025-03-05. We used the official acute-treatment summary and original position-paper abstract; not an unread full-text claim.
- Italy: [SISC index](https://www.sisc.it/ita/linee-guida-diagnostico-terapeutiche-delle-cefalee-primarie_33.html), SISC/IHS 2025 joint guideline. The published summary tables were reviewed. Recommendation strength is not treatment-line order or AIFA reimbursement policy.
- Spain: [SEN 2026 manual](https://web.sen.es/profesionales/guias-y-protocolos/3843-manual-de-practica-clinica-en-cefaleas-edicion-2026), fifth edition; migraine acute chapter and preventive algorithm.
- Netherlands: [NVN specialist migraine modules](https://richtlijnendatabase.nl/richtlijn/hoofdpijn/migraine.html); the comparison specifically uses the CGRP-antibody module dated 2023-12-04. It does not claim that other modules or primary-care guidance have the same date.
- Denmark: [NNBV migraine](https://nnbv.dk/migraene-behandling/) updated 2026-09-19; separate CGRP chapter fifth edition 2026-07-28. The comparison does not reproduce every listed drug/indication, and keeps treatment eligibility in a separate column.
- Switzerland: [SKG therapy commission](https://www.headache.ch/portrait/therapiekommission) links **12th edition**, copyright 2026. The older downloads page/search snippets mentioning edition 11.2 are not the current linked edition.
- Finland: [Käypä hoito](https://www.kaypahoito.fi/hoi36050) dated 2024-03-28, marked under revision. Planned 2026 updates are not published guidance.
- Cross-border: EHF 2022 CGRP guideline (first-line positioning is expert consensus, Table 9); EAN cluster 2023 and TN 2019 (original abstracts). These are not substitutes for country-specific recommendations or drug access rules.

## Data contract and review process

Each source records a publisher, scope, version date (year/month/day only as precisely known), document type, stable HTTPS source, review date, reading depth and version caveat. Each comparison is one jurisdiction plus one syndrome. Every summarized domain requires a source ID and locator; `not_covered` means outside that document's scope, while `not_verified` means no current verified comparison is available. Administrative conditions quoted from older documents are explicitly historical.

To update: check the official source and its supersession status, read the relevant recommendation and qualifying text, update only the affected claims/versions, and preserve exact provenance. Never infer a national position from an EHF/EAN paper. Do not treat a drug authorization, funding decision or position statement as a comprehensive guideline. Keep adult summaries concise and avoid adding unsourced doses or extrapolating to pregnancy/children.

The UI displays the review date, warns after 90 days, shows explicit country-topic gaps, and links each claim to its source. It never labels daily paper synchronization as a fresh guideline review. Content uses original concise paraphrases; source PDFs are not redistributed in this repository.

## Validation

`node --test tests/*.test.cjs` validates source references, source URLs, version dates, coverage, treatment/access separation, filter intersections, stale-data notices and the service worker's preservation of verified cached data on invalid network responses. `python -m pytest -q` exercises the existing pipeline without external API calls. Manual browser verification covers mobile layout, independent guideline failure/retry, source expansion, offline data and the existing read/star controls.
